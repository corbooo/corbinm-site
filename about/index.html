I'm from League City, Texas, and I'm currently studying Computer Science at the University of Houston–Clear Lake. My college journey started at Texas A&M Galveston before I transferred to UHCL, where I've been continuing to explore my interest in technology, problem-solving, and building things of my own.

I enjoy turning ideas into something real, whether that's a useful application, a small game, or a new design. I've worked on projects ranging from fitness-related tools to interactive software, and lately I've been exploring graphic design and animation too. I like the challenge of making something that doesn't just work, but also looks and feels good to use.

When I'm not coding, I'm usually finding something else to keep me busy. My other hobbies include staying active, typically by working out, following my favorite sporting teams, experimenting with new tech, and the occasional video game.

I'm working toward graduating with my Computer Science degree in 2027, and I'm excited to start applying what I've learned in the real world. I want to keep improving as a developer and creating things that people genuinely enjoy using all while exploring what the newest technology has to offer. There's still so much I must learn, and I'm looking forward to seeing where it takes me.
